## Results

(claude-6; branch `task/L50`)

**1. Boot stop 17 (handed off first, #9784).** The rule is
`lift.jumper_state`, part of `frame_tail`. An entry reached by a direct
jump is the jumper's code when, on some path, either holds:
- it reads ebx, esi, edi or ebp before writing it. Under the MSVC x86
  conventions these are callee-saved, never inputs of a called function.
- it pops below, or releases, the stack its jumper set up.

These are not reads:
- saves: `push` or a dword store, as `___report_gsfailure` does;
- no-op padding: `mov edi, edi`, `lea ebx, [ebx]`;
- writes that do not depend on the register: `xor`/`sub r, r`,
  `or r, -1`, `and r, 0`;
- ebp set by a call to `__SEH_prolog4`.

Before those exclusions the rule matched 85 records; after them, exactly
the census. The decoder, the emitter, the x87 walk, fragments and the gate
all use `frame_tail`, so a record that still leaves its body this way is
reported as "stale body" or "tail call into its own inlined code".
- **Census targets, now decoded into their jumpers:**
  - 0x01146420 (reads edi) into 0x01146410;
  - 0x006F3242 (releases its jumper's `sub esp, 8`) into 0x006F3230;
  - 0x00947493 (reads edi) into 0x009472C0;
  - 0x01137DF3 (reads edi) into 0x01137DD0.
- **Refreshed:** the four records, each with three added cases that run
  the inlined code (small-v1, random). The old cases never took the jump.
- **Credited as fragments** of their verified jumpers: the four targets.
- **Not this rule:** 0x012F693F, 0x012F6A6F and 0x01300DFF read no jumper
  state. They are real tail calls into entries without records (the
  recorder lane).
- **Also covered:** the CRT shared tail 0x0130070B (pops below its entry)
  is decoded into 0x012F693F, 0x012F6A6F, 0x01329888 and 0x012F6948
  (Codex #9802).
- **Tests:** a jumper that pushes edi and sets it inlines the target that
  reads and pops it. A target with its own frame (and hot-patch padding)
  stays a tail call. A bare `pop` below the entry is inlined.

**2. x87 mock pops.**
- **The model.** A mocked direct call pops the x87 values its callee
  takes, then pushes where `returns_float` proves a result, as before.
  - The callee's contract (`mock_pops`) is `x87_deficit` over its own
    body. An entry that only moves registers and jumps on (the `_CI`
    stubs) takes its target's contract.
  - In the deficit walk, a mocked callee that takes values reads them and
    ends the path, as a return does.
  - A callee that takes nothing and has no calls is walked past with its
    proven exit depth (`mock_leaves`). 0x013048CD loads one argument this
    way; 0x0130470B loads two such arguments before `call 0x01300497`,
    which takes 2. A callee with calls cannot prove what it leaves:
    0x00D53F30 returns its own callee's float, which no mock inside it
    pushes.
- **The recorder** returns a mocked call through a sled of eight
  `fstp st(0)` on the trampoline page. It records `st_pop` in the call
  event, and records carry `x87_pop` ([return address, n]) through
  BODY_INFO.
- **The replay:** `X87::pop` in ddnative-base; the handlers in the
  lift.py template, ddnative's `lifted.rs` and ddperf pop before
  `st_push`. Vectors without `st_pop` replay as before.
- **The x87 depth walk** now visits every depth that reaches an
  instruction (at most nine), so a loop that grows the stack meets the
  overflow check.
- **Footprint.** Of 3,961 ext records with x87 work, 168 change:
  - 112 gain pop sites only;
  - 54 gain pop sites and change push sites;
  - 2 change push sites only (0x00DD05F0, 0x00FC28A0).

  None is rejected, and the 14,204 closed records are unchanged.
- **Refresh.** `refresh` now also takes a named record whose recording
  contract (its vector key) the current lifter changes. 165 of the 168
  were refreshed with their previous distributions; 161 records now carry
  pop sites and 257 cases record `st_pop`. Not refreshable, and left as
  they were: 0x007128B0, 0x00712D90 and 0x00A68400. Their old cases are
  unseeded random ones, and today's random distribution runs outside the
  image with or without pops. Gen will see their contract change and
  re-record them, so they may be lost unless an input strategy reaches
  them (told Codex).
- **New functions:** 0x009897C0, 0x00F83C00 and 0x013156B0 record 3/3
  (small-v1) and are adopted.
- **Tests:** a loop through a callee that takes one value lifts with one
  pop site. Without pops it overflows statically. In the recorder each
  call records `st_pop` 1 and the stack ends empty (3 without the pops).

**3. Frame slots across calls (`frame_clean`).** A memory slot may cross a
call to a callee whose every memory write, through its direct callees too
(three deep), lies at or below its own return address and argument bytes.
The writes must be esp- or ebp-relative at a tracked stack depth, and
proven tables are followed. An esp slot moves by the callee's `ret` pop;
a callee-saved base stays as it is. This is proven from the lifted code,
with no object-model assumption.

It does not close the two gaps, so they stay gaps:
- **0x009921E0:** 0x0098FC80 (frame-clean, pops 4) and 0x00770EE0 (no
  writes) lie on the direct path. But the loop back-edge at 0x009927C2
  brings in `call 0x00992C00`, which writes through object pointers.
- **0x00A2F3B0:** 0x004241F0 and 0x00429680 receive frame addresses
  (`lea eax, [esp + 0x10]`) and write through them. Proving those writes
  stay in their pointees needs an escape and bounds analysis over their
  callees.

The rule changes no table proof or body across the 2,199 table records.
Tests: a slot bound before `push eax; call` to a callee that writes only
its locals; a callee that writes through ecx breaks it.
